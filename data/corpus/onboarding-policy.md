# Employee Onboarding Policy

## Purpose

This policy describes how Northwind Supply onboards a new employee, from signed offer
letter through the end of the first ninety days. It applies to every full time hire in
the United States.

## Day One Checklist

The hiring manager must complete the following before the new hire's first day:

- Request hardware from IT at least five business days in advance. A standard laptop
  build takes three business days to image.
- Create accounts in the identity provider and add the hire to their department group.
- Assign a peer buddy from the same team who has been with the company at least six
  months.
- Book a thirty minute welcome call for 9:30 am on the first day.

New hires receive their equipment on day one. If equipment has not arrived, the
manager must file a delay ticket and arrange a loaner within twenty four hours.

## Probation and Reviews

Every new employee is subject to a ninety day probationary period. The manager runs a
formal check in at thirty days, sixty days and ninety days. The thirty day check in is
informal and does not require written documentation. The sixty and ninety day reviews
must be documented in the HR system within five business days of the conversation.

An employee who does not pass the ninety day review may have probation extended once,
by a maximum of thirty additional days, with written approval from the department head
and a member of the people team.

## Benefits Enrollment

Health, dental and vision coverage begins on the first day of the month following the
start date. Enrollment must be completed within thirty days of the start date. An
employee who misses the enrollment window waits until the next open enrollment period
unless they have a qualifying life event.

The company matches retirement contributions up to four percent of base salary. The
match vests over two years of continuous service.

## Remote and Hybrid Employees

Remote employees follow the same onboarding checklist with two changes. Equipment
ships to the home address and must be tracked with a signature on delivery. The peer
buddy schedules two additional video check ins during the first two weeks, because
remote hires report lower connection scores at the thirty day mark.
